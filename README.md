## My First Repository

My favorite series is **Kamen Rider**, especially **Kamen Rider Geats**.
Kamen Rider Geats is about a survival game called the *Desire Grand Prix (DGP)*. Players receive a card where they write their ideal world, and the winner makes that world come true. The story follows Ace Ukiyo (Geats), a smart, strong, and cunning character who believes that if you keep wishing, it will come true someday. He fights to uncover the truth behind the DGP and search for his long-lost mother.

This is just one of many stories in the Kamen Rider series.
